Flow 01

Order to cash

  1. Capture demandCommerce or EDI order contract
  2. Validate and priceNetSuite customer, item, tax, and approval controls
  3. Fulfill and confirm3PL release, shipment, and acknowledgement
  4. Invoice and settleAccounts receivable, payment, and bank evidence

Flow 02

Procure to pay

  1. Authorize demandRequisition and approval boundary
  2. Commit supplyPurchase order and vendor contract
  3. Receive and reconcileItem receipt, bill, and three-way match
  4. Release paymentAuthorization, bank file, and settlement evidence

Flow 03

Fulfillment

  1. Allocate inventoryLocation, lot, and promise controls
  2. Release the order3PL or warehouse instruction contract
  3. Confirm movementPick, pack, ship, and carrier events
  4. Reconcile exceptionsShort, damage, delay, and retry evidence

Flow 04

Financial close

  1. Bind the periodSubsidiary, accounting book, and close scope
  2. Reconcile boundariesBank, commerce, inventory, and intercompany controls
  3. Resolve exceptionsOwnership, evidence, and approval trail
  4. Publish confidenceReporting, variance, and audit-ready outcome
